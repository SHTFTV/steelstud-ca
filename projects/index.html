NEW WESTMINSTER · LAGUNA PROJECT

Dropped condo ceiling for recessed lighting.

This New Westminster condo ceiling was lowered to create space for recessed pot lights while maintaining a clean finished ceiling plane. In concrete and high-rise residential construction, a new ceiling cavity can provide the service zone required for lighting and electrical coordination.

The assembly creates room above the finished drywall while allowing the lighting layout and ceiling geometry to read as one coordinated installation.
