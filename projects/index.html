NEW WESTMINSTER · LAGUNA PROJECT

Dropped condo ceiling for recessed lighting.

This condo ceiling was lowered to create the space needed for recessed pot lights while maintaining a clean finished ceiling plane. The work illustrates a common renovation problem in concrete and high-rise residential construction: lighting and services need room above the finished drywall, but the existing structure may not provide a conventional ceiling cavity.

The solution is a coordinated dropped ceiling assembly that creates a service zone, provides a level drywall surface and lets the lighting layout read cleanly across the room.

PROJECT KNOWLEDGE

Why these details matter before work starts.

Ceiling depth

Dropped ceilings and clouds need enough depth for framing, fixtures, wiring, ducts and access without forcing awkward field changes.

Lighting coordination

Pot lights, linear lights and other fixtures should be laid out with the framing and finished ceiling geometry rather than cut in as an afterthought.

Service integration

T-bar and custom ceilings often share space with HVAC, sprinklers, speakers and access panels. Early layout makes the finished ceiling look intentional.
